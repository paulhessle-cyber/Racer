⚙ Train Model

v3: learns what each signal adds beyond the starting price. Every race is scored using only results from earlier days (no look-ahead), with: horse win and place rate, jockey and trainer win rate, draw by course, going, distance and course record, freshness, Official Rating, the horse's previous RPR and Topspeed, and recent form. The newest 20% of races are held back to test it against the market alone. The app only uses the model if it beat the market on that test.
