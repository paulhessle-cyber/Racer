What does it do?

RaceIQ scores every horse using 12+ signals and ranks them by predicted finishing position. It uses the market as an anchor and adjusts based on form, pace, jockey, trainer, and race dynamics.

📊 Market anchor

True implied probability (overround removed) anchors every prediction. 40% weight — the dominant signal.

📈 Recent form

Last 3 runs weighted by recency: most recent 50%, second 30%, third 20%.

🧑 Jockey + Trainer

35+ elite jockeys and trainers carry multipliers. 15 specific jockey-trainer pairings get combo bonuses.

🏃 Pace & draw

Front-runner vs closer detection. Draw position interacts with field size and pace scenario.

⚠️
This is a tool, not a guarantee. Racing is unpredictable. Gamble responsibly.
2
Making a prediction
  1. Download the free form guide PDF from attheraces.com for your meeting.
  2. Tap Races, load the PDF — all runners extract automatically.
  3. Tap the race, then go to Predict.
  4. Enter decimal odds for each runner (or paste them — see section 3).
  5. Set the going, tap Predict This Race.
💰
Decimal odds: 7/2 = (7÷2)+1 = 4.5. Evens = 2.0. Enter as close to race time as possible.
ℹ️
No odds entered? The app will warn you and switch to form-only mode. Without odds it is little better than sorting by Official Rating.
3
Pasting odds from ATR

Instead of typing odds one by one, copy the entire runners section from the attheraces.com race page and paste it in one go.

  1. On ATR, select all the runner text for the race and copy it.
  2. In the Predict tab, tap 📋 Paste Odds at the top of the Runners card.
  3. Paste the text and tap ✓ Apply Odds.
💡
Steam & Drift detection: Paste odds a second time nearer the off and the app automatically detects which horses have shortened (▲ Steam) or drifted (▽ Drift) by 10%+. Steamers and drifters feed directly into the scoring.

Reading the results
🏆 Predicted Winner

Top-scoring horse with confidence % relative to the field.

💰 Value Bet

A top-3 pick at 9.0 or longer — model rates it worth a look at those odds.

📊 Model vs Market

Bar chart showing your model % vs market implied % for each horse. Green ▲ VALUE = model rates higher. Red ▼ AVOID = market rates higher.

💡 Score breakdown

Under each horse in the order: "Market 18% · Jockey +9% · Combo +7%" shows what drove the score.

🎯
The ▲ VALUE flag means the model rates that horse meaningfully above what its price implies. It is a prompt to look closer, not an instruction to back it.
5
Bet Advisor & Dutching

After predicting, the Bet Advisor shows the expected value (EV) for Win, Each Way, and Place bets at your chosen stake — and recommends which is best.

💰 Dutching

If 2–3 horses score above 8%, the app shows exact stakes to split your money so any one of them winning returns the same profit.

1/4 vs 1/5

Toggle place terms to match your bookmaker. All EV figures update instantly.

ℹ️
EV explained: Expected value is what you would make on average over many identical bets. Negative EV = losing bet long term even if it wins today.
6
Pace analysis

The app reads form figures to classify each horse's running style, then assesses the whole race.

🟠 Front-runner

Shows 1 or 2 early in recent form. Gets a bonus as lone leader; penalised in contested pace.

🟣 Closer

Finishes strongly from behind. Benefits when pace is contested; penalised in slow-run races.

💡
Draw position interacts with pace style. A low draw suits front-runners in big fields; a high draw can cause traffic problems for closers.
7
Jockey & Trainer Intelligence

35+ elite UK and Irish jockeys carry win-rate multipliers. 35+ top trainers carry separate multipliers. When a top jockey rides for a top trainer, an additional combo bonus applies on top of both.

💡
Examples of combo bonuses: Buick/Appleby, Moore/O'Brien, Dettori/Gosden, Murphy/Balding. These pairings historically outperform their individual win rates.
ℹ️
Trainer names are not in the ATR PDF so trainer bonuses only apply when using a trained model that has trainer data from your CSV.
8
Placepot

The Placepot tab analyses all six races and suggests banker picks, alternative selections, and line combinations.

  1. Load your PDF and enter odds in the Predict tab for each race first.
  2. Tap Placepot — picks generate automatically.
  3. Tap ⚡ Generate Picks to refresh after changing the going.
  4. Tap 🤖 Second Opinion for a form-only critique of each pick.
🎯
Second Opinion: Runs a separate form-only analysis (no market) and compares it against the placepot picks. Where both agree: Confirmed banker. Where they differ: Caution or Value Flag with the alternative horse named.

Setup & trained model

The app works without a model using default weights. Load a trained racing_model.json for significantly better accuracy — it adds horse win rates, course records, going preferences, jockey and trainer stats from your historical data.

  1. Use the desktop Trainer app to build a model from your CSV race history.
  2. Transfer racing_model.json to your phone via AirDrop, WhatsApp, or email.
  3. Tap Setup, then load the file. Green confirmation appears when loaded.
ℹ️
Once you load a PDF, the Setup tab shows the racecourse and detected surface (Turf/AW/Jumps) and tells you whether your loaded model matches.

Common questions
❓ PDF loaded but no runners appeared
The app reads At The Races form guide PDFs only. PDFs from Racing Post or bookmakers use different formats.
❓ All horses show the same percentage
Odds have not been entered. The market is the dominant signal — without odds all horses get equal market weight and results are unreliable.
❓ Why doesn't the top horse show 40–50% confidence?
By design. Even the clearest favourite in UK racing wins 25–30% of the time. Scores above that are misleading.
❓ The prediction looks like odds order
When form signals are similar across the field, the market is the best available guide and the model will largely agree with it. Meaningful divergence happens when recent form, pace scenario, jockey-trainer combos, or steam/drift create a genuine edge.
❓ Does it work for jump racing?
The model was trained on UK and Irish flat turf only. It can rank jump runners but accuracy will be lower.
❓ Is my data private?
Yes — everything runs locally on your phone. No server, no account, no data sent anywhere. localStorage only.
