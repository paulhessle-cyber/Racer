What does it do?

The Predictor scores every horse in a race and ranks them in order of predicted finishing position. Scores are based on a trained model but are calibrated to stay realistic — even clear favourites rarely exceed 25–28% in a competitive field.

🐎 The horse

Win rate, going and distance preference, course record — ranked relative to the field.

🧑 Jockey & trainer

Individual win rates from thousands of rides, ranked within the field.

📊 Market odds

SP is a strong signal — given more weight than in older versions.

🏃 Pace shape

Front-runner detection from form figures. Lone leaders get a boost; contested pace penalises all speed horses.

⚠️
This is a tool, not a guarantee. Racing is unpredictable. Use it to inform your thinking, not replace it. Gamble responsibly.
2
Pace analysis

The app analyses each runner's form figures to classify their likely running style and then assesses the pace shape of the whole race.

🟠 Front-runner

Shows a "1" or "2" early in most recent form figures. Gets a bonus if it will be the lone leader.

🟣 Closer

Typically finishes strongly from behind. Gets a slight bonus when the pace scenario is hot/contested.

💡
Pace analysis is based only on finishing positions in the form string. It's a signal, not a certainty — horses can be ridden differently on the day.
3
Making a prediction
  1. Go to attheraces.com, find your race, and download the form guide PDF.
  2. Tap the Races tab, then load the PDF. All runners are extracted automatically.
  3. Tap the race you want to predict, then tap Predict.
  4. Enter decimal odds for each runner, set the going, then tap Predict This Race.
💰
Decimal odds: 7/2 = (7÷2)+1 = 4.5. Evens = 2.0. Enter as close to race time as possible.

Common questions
❓ PDF loaded but no runners appeared
The app reads At The Races form guide PDFs only.
❓ All horses show the same score
Odds haven't been entered yet — they're a big part of the scoring. Enter decimal odds and the scores will spread out.
❓ Why doesn't the top horse show 40–50% confidence?
By design. Even the clearest standout in UK racing wins ~25–30% of the time. The model is temperature-scaled to stay realistic.
❓ Does it work for jump racing?
The model was trained on UK and Irish flat turf only. It can rank runners but accuracy will be lower for jumps.
